    How the Mediator works

    1 · Profile

    Each person's loved titles become signals for Qloo's taste analysis — returning the tags that describe their taste.

    2 · Discover

    Everyone's signals are combined across film, TV, music, books and places — Qloo's cross-domain graph connects a record to a restaurant.

    3 · Be fair

    Each candidate is re-scored against every person alone. Ranking is least-misery first, so nobody is dragged somewhere they'd hate.

    4 · Explain

    Every pick shows who loves it, how much, and which of your favourites it echoes.
